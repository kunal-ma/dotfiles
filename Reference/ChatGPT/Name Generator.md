# Project Name Generator

> Suggest a suitable name for the app based on the description I provide.

## Specifications

The name should be:

- **Easy to say and spell** — simple, natural, and memorable when heard or spoken.
- **Quirky and distinctive** — have enough personality to stand out without becoming confusing or gimmicky.
- **Relevant** — subtly communicate or evoke the problem the app solves, its core functionality, or the value it provides.
- **Short and brandable** — preferably 1–2 words, with a strong potential to work as a product or startup name.
- **Memorable** — avoid generic, overly descriptive, or forgettable names.
- **Versatile** — the name should still make sense if the app expands its features or audience later.
- **Original in feel** — avoid obvious clichés, common SaaS naming patterns, or names that sound too similar to well-known products.

## Output

Give me **10 name suggestions**, ranked from strongest to weakest.

For each name, provide:

1. **Name**
2. **Why it works** — a brief explanation connecting the name to the app.
3. **Name style** — e.g. playful, clever, premium, futuristic, minimal, technical, friendly, etc.
4. **Pronunciation** — only if the pronunciation isn't immediately obvious.

After the list, identify:

- **Top 3 picks**
- **Best overall name**
- **One-sentence rationale** for why it is the strongest choice

Do not limit your creativity to literal descriptions of the app. You may use wordplay, metaphors, unexpected combinations, invented words, cultural references, or clever abbreviations, provided the result remains easy to pronounce, memorable, and relevant.
